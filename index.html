FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency and fast dental care in Gaithersburg, MD.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe toothache, a knocked-out or cracked tooth, a dental abscess, swelling in the face or gums, uncontrolled bleeding and injuries to the mouth are all reasons to seek urgent care. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you decide.

Do you offer same day emergency dental appointments?

Yes. We focus on fast dental care and make every effort to see urgent patients the same day. Call 240-224-8082 so we can arrange your visit as quickly as possible.

What should I do if my tooth is knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, not the root, and gently rinse it without scrubbing. Try to place it back in the socket, or keep it in milk, and call us right away. Acting within the first hour gives the best chance of saving the tooth.

Can you treat a dental abscess or severe infection?

Yes. A dental abscess needs prompt attention because infection can spread. We can evaluate the problem and provide treatment such as drainage, a root canal or an extraction, depending on your situation. If you have fever or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We aim to keep emergency care affordable and will explain your treatment options and costs before we begin. Call our office to ask about payment and insurance questions.
